# Security

DeskID is a prototype. The known limits of the current hardware are listed under "Security limits" in [README.md](README.md).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through GitHub: open the repository's Security tab and choose "Report a vulnerability". Please keep vulnerability details out of public issues.

A useful report names the commit, the build environment (`deskid`, `deskid-bringup` or `deskid-production`), the steps to reproduce and what an attacker gains.

## Disclosure

Please allow 90 days from your report before publishing, or less if a fix ships sooner. You are free to publish afterwards, and credit is yours unless you ask otherwise.

## Research

Permission 3 in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) lets you change the firmware and tools privately for good-faith research on hardware you own or are authorised to test. Research has to stay off other people's devices, accounts and data.
